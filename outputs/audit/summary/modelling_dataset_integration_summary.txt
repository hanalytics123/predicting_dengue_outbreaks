INTEGRATION METHOD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dengue is the master table because each dengue row defines a prediction observation. PERSIANN precipitation, CFS temperature/humidity, CFS wind, CFS surface pressure and MODIS NDVI are left-joined on week_start_date.
All environmental candidate predictors and QA/completeness fields are retained at this stage. No feature selection or dropping of QA fields is performed during integration.
