INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Prefer a split that leaves enough historical observations for training while giving validation and test periods meaningful variation in dengue burden.
2. The final test period should remain untouched during model selection and hyperparameter tuning.
3. Differences in MAE/RMSE can be strongly affected by epidemic peaks because the target is highly right-skewed.
4. Feature eligibility should be considered separately from target availability. Models using fewer features may legitimately retain more observations.
5. This script does not choose a split automatically. The final choice should be documented as a methodological decision before model fitting.
